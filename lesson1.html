Lesson 1: Introduction to Cybersecurity

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, websites, networks and information from digital threats.

Why is it important?

It helps protect personal information, online accounts and digital systems.

Four important topics

1. Password security

2. Network security

3. Website security

4. Data protection

Ethical Hacking

Ethical hacking means testing computer security with permission to find weaknesses and help fix them.

Quick Quiz

What is the main purpose of cybersecurity?

A. Stealing information

B. Protecting digital systems

C. Damaging websites

Correct answer: B
